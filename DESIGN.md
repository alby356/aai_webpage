# Visual direction
Centered introduction and direct project links, informed by Foundable's simple focal point and Uncodixfy's restrained UI.
Space Grotesk, neutral charcoal #121212, surface #1a1a1a, text #f5f5f5, secondary #aaa9ae, border #333235, muted violet #c9b5eb.
Six projects share a compact responsive grid. Standard links styled as buttons; no client JavaScript. Visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support.
