# Visual direction
Compact subject directory with a modest page title, subject filters, and alphabetical sorting. Separate club page with factual content. No large hero or slogans.
Space Grotesk, neutral charcoal #121212, surface #1a1a1a, text #f5f5f5, secondary #aaa9ae, border #333235, muted violet #c9b5eb.
Six projects share a compact responsive grid. Chemistry and English each have one shared subject icon. Native same-origin page transitions and a 360ms new-tab handoff with an immediate link. Skeletons only while the handoff module initializes. Visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, and explicit empty/invalid-link states.
